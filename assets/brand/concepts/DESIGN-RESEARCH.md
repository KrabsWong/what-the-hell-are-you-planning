# Third set: researched identity directions

Reviewed 2026-10-31. These references inform design principles; none of their artwork is included in the SVG files.

## References

- Pentagram, Intrinsic: https://www.pentagram.com/work/intrinsic — functional, robust identity; a generative motion tool expresses software behavior. Takeaway: encode the product process in motion.
- Variable, Hebbia Motion Tool: https://variable.io/hebbia-motion-tool/ — a constrained, editable grid system becomes a scalable motion identity. Takeaway: few modules and explicit rules, with stable resting states.
- BP&O, Werf by andstudio: https://bpando.org/2026/10/27/andstudio-werf-rotterdam-bar-identity-design/ — abstraction of a specific angle into a coherent brand device. Takeaway: a simple geometric rule should have a conceptual reason.

## Original explorations

09 Query / Cut: custom angular question mark; questioning as the core of the product name. A lateral registration movement and turning full stop; 7.2 seconds.
10 Counterform: opposing folded chevrons form a central void; independent evidence aligns. Paired horizontal movement; 7 seconds.
11 Folio / P: custom P with detached bowl and folded page corner; Planning and documents. Registration and folding; 7.5 seconds.
12 Assembly: four offset bars form a square system with an open center; organization and execution. Explode, settle, hold; 8 seconds.

All four work in one ink. Their CSS `--logo-ink` custom property can override the default warm gold when embedded. Transparent 128×128 viewBox, CSS keyframes, no JavaScript or external files inside SVG. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation. Native app animation integration remains a separate step.
